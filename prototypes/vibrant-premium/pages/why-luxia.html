Why “Luxia Prevention & Coaching”?

Luxia Prevention & Coaching was created from a desire to build a space that supports people and organizations in their conscious development and in creating a safer future.

I believe that every person deserves to feel heard, respected, and valued. That is why Luxia brings together two areas that are especially close to my heart: coaching that supports personal growth, and prevention and workplace safety services that help create healthy and responsible working environments.

It is a place for individuals and businesses that want to act with purpose, unlock their potential, strengthen collaboration, and build a culture based on trust, responsibility, and mutual support.
