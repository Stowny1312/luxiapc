A new chapter

Courage, perseverance, and new beginnings

In 2014, I began a new chapter in Belgium together with my children. Like many people, I faced the challenges of learning a new language, adapting to a different culture, and finding my place in a new country.

This journey taught me courage, perseverance, and the belief that even the biggest changes can become the beginning of something beautiful.

People have always been my greatest passion. Through years of professional and personal experience, I have learned that every individual possesses enormous potential. Sometimes, all we need is a little support, understanding, and encouragement to help that potential flourish.

02

Leading people

Connection creates possibility

Today, as a Supervisor and Team Leader, I lead a diverse team of people from different cultures and backgrounds. Every day, I witness how much can be achieved through collaboration, mutual respect, and open communication.

I firmly believe that when people feel heard, valued, and connected to a shared purpose, they can surpass their own expectations and accomplish more than they ever thought possible.

My mission

A safe space for growth and change

My mission is to create a space where people can discover their strengths, build self-confidence, and realize that change, even when challenging, can open the door to new opportunities.

This is why I am so passionate about the power of support, connection, and personal growth.

“Luxia Prevention & Coaching” was born from my passion for people and my belief in their potential. It is a place created for those who want to rediscover their inner strength, regain confidence, better understand themselves, and embrace change with courage.

Perhaps someone just like you.

Outside of work

The meaning behind the name

Where does “Luxia” come from?

The name Luxia was inspired by the concepts of light, clarity, and guidance that we often seek during times of change, uncertainty, and new beginnings. It symbolizes a space where you can pause, reflect, and gain a fresh perspective on your journey.

For me, Luxia stands for clarity, growth, and conscious action. It is a place designed to help people discover new possibilities, deepen their self-awareness, and take meaningful steps toward the life they truly want to create.

Whether you are navigating personal challenges, pursuing professional growth, or seeking greater balance and fulfillment, Luxia is here to support and inspire your journey.

Like a light that illuminates the path ahead, Luxia Prevention & Coaching helps you find direction, build confidence, and move forward with courage and purpose.

Because every transformation begins with a moment of clarity.

Luxia. Clarity. Growth. Possibility.
