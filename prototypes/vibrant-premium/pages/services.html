01

Workplace Prevention & Safety

Creating a safe workplace is more than meeting legal requirements. It is about building an environment where people can perform at their best with confidence and peace of mind.

Designed for Group C and Group D organizations, in accordance with Belgian legislation and the Codex on Well-being at Work.

  • Risk assessments and workplace audits
  • Developing and strengthening a proactive safety culture
  • Support in creating policies, procedures, and safety standards
  • Preventive measures that reduce workplace risks and incidents
  • Practical guidance tailored to your organization's needs
