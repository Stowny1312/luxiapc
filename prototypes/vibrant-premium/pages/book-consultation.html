Individual Coaching Packages & Investment

Time, commitment, and the right support.

Meaningful change takes time, commitment, and the right support. While many clients notice positive shifts and greater clarity after just 4 to 6 coaching sessions, creating lasting habits or achieving more complex goals often requires a deeper coaching journey.

For this reason, I generally recommend a coaching program of 6 to 10 sessions, allowing enough time to explore challenges, develop effective strategies, build confidence, and create sustainable changes in everyday life.

The exact number of sessions will always depend on your individual goals and circumstances. During our first conversation, we will discuss what you would like to achieve and determine the most suitable approach for your journey.

Each 60-minute session is dedicated entirely to you.
